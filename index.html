About Tux Interior

Built on Craftsmanship, Delivered with Integrity

Tux Interior started in 2026 in Ahmedabad with a focus on creating modern, practical and beautifully finished interiors.

The company provides complete interior solutions from planning and design to execution and final finishing. Our team combines practical execution experience, carpentry knowledge, material understanding and meticulous project coordination to make your home journey smooth and stress-free.

  • ✦ Practical on-site execution with direct supervision on every residential site.
  • ✦ In-depth material guidance for plywood, laminates, acrylics, hardware and paints.
  • ✦ Timely schedules and clear communication for homeowners across Ahmedabad.

Our Services

End-to-End Home Interior Solutions

From initial site measurement to final polish, we cover every aspect of residential interior execution under one roof.

1. Complete Home Interior

Holistic interior design and turnkey finishing tailored for modern 2 BHK, 3 BHK, and 4 BHK residences in Ahmedabad.

2. Turnkey Interior Projects

Zero-stress project management. We handle procurement, vendor coordination, labor, and quality checks right to final keys.

3. Furniture & Carpentry

Custom on-site carpentry with calibrated plywood, durable laminates, premium edge-banding, and precision woodcraft.

4. Modular Furniture

Factory-finished modular wardrobes, TV consoles, and study units equipped with soft-close hydraulic hardware.

5. POP & False Ceiling

Contemporary gypsum and POP false ceiling patterns with concealed cove channels and architectural profiles.

6. Painting & Wall Finishes

Royal luster finishes, textured accent walls, fluted charcoal paneling, and durable PU coats for exquisite walls.

7. Electrical Work

Safe concealed wiring, modular switch installations, ambient architectural lighting, and two-way bedside controls.

8. Home Décor

Curated artifacts, mirror panel accents, custom curtains, lighting fixtures, and tasteful styling accessories.

9. Bedroom Interiors

Serene master and kids bedrooms featuring upholstered headboards, bedside niches, and reeded glass wardrobes.

10. Living Room Interiors

Warm, welcoming living areas with custom TV units, entrance foyers, mandir partitions, and cozy sofa seating.

11. Kitchen Interiors

Ergonomic modular kitchens featuring acrylic/laminate shutters, tandem drawers, corner pullouts, and quartz counters.

12. Storage Solutions

Clever concealed lofts, shoe consoles, vanity units, and utility storage maximizing space in modern apartments.

How We Work

Our 6-Step Turnkey Process

A structured, hassle-free journey designed to give you clarity and complete peace of mind at each stage.

01

Consultation

Understanding your vision, lifestyle needs, preferred styles, and initial budget parameters.

02

Site Visit

Comprehensive on-site measurements, architectural structural check, and lighting assessment.

03

Design & Planning

2D layout optimization, furniture ergonomics, false ceiling layouts, and electrical point planning.

04

Material Selection

Curating laminates, veneers, hardware, countertop stones, and paint shades with tangible samples.

05

Execution

Hands-on carpentry, ceiling installation, tiling, electrical work, and painting with milestone reviews.

06

Final Handover

Deep cleaning, hardware inspection, final walkthrough, and handover of your ready-to-live home.
