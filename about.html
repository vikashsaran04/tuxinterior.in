Our Story

How Tux Interior Came to Life

Tux Interior started in 2026 in Ahmedabad with a simple yet resolute focus: creating modern, practical, and beautifully finished interiors without the confusion, delays, and miscommunication that so many homeowners experience during renovation.

Having observed that many interior firms focus solely on digital renders while neglecting the realities of on-site woodwork and craftsmanship, our founders set out to build a studio anchored in real-world execution.

We provide complete turnkey solutions from initial layout planning and 3D visualization right through to structural carpentry, modular kitchen installations, false ceilings, electrical choreography, and final surface polishing.

Execution Workflow

The Turnkey Journey

Six transparent stages from first phone call to the moment you step into your completed home.

01

Consultation

Deep-dive discussion on lifestyle needs, taste, timeline, and preliminary budget.

02

Site Visit

Physical inspection of the apartment or bungalow to assess site layout and MEP lines.

03

Design & Planning

Detailed 2D drawings, furniture space allocations, and lighting diagrams.

04

Material Selection

Tangible review of wood grades, edge bands, hardware brands, and fabrics.

05

Execution

On-site precision carpentry, ceiling framing, electric work, and painting.

06

Final Handover

Thorough cleaning, hardware adjustment, and key handover ready for possession.
